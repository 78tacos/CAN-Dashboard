Here are some additional things commonly found on a CAN bus that can be visually illustrated in your dashboard:

1. **Door Status** (open/closed for each door, with icons)
2. **Headlights/Signal Lights** (on/off, left/right indicators)
3. **Brake Status** (brake applied/released, brake pressure)
4. **Odometer/Trip Meter** (distance traveled)
5. **Engine RPM** (tachometer gauge)
6. **Fuel Level** (gauge or bar, for diesel/electric hybrids)
7. **Interior/Exterior Temperature** (thermometer icon)
8. **HVAC Fan Speed** (fan icon, bar or dial)
9. **Wheel Speed Sensors** (individual wheel speeds)
10. **Fault/Error Codes** (warning icons, error messages)
11. **GPS Location/Speed** (map or coordinates, if available)
12. **Passenger Count** (for public transport, with people icons)
13. **Battery Health/State of Charge** (for electric vehicles, detailed battery info)
14. **Current Gear** (gear indicator for automatic/manual)
15. **Traction Control/ABS Status** (indicator lights)
16. **Horn Activation** (icon flashes when horn is pressed)
17. **Wiper Status** (on/off, speed)
18. **Charging Status** (for EVs, plug icon, charging animation)
19. **Cabin Lights** (on/off, dim level)
20. **Speed Limit Recognition** (detected speed limit sign)

All of these can be represented with icons, gauges, bars, or animations for a more interactive and informative dashboard.
